A global client-side caching architecture for DAOS (Distributed Asynchronous Object Storage) that aggregates DRAM, SSDs, and network bandwidth from compute nodes to eliminate server-side bottlenecks. Enables orders-of-magnitude improvements in data access latency and throughput for representative HPC and AI workloads such as PageRank.

**Authors:** C.R. Crasta, J.L. Byrne, A. Dwaraki, D. Emberson, H.A. Kuno, S. Lee, et al.

**Venue:** Proceedings of the Cray User Group (CUG 2025), pp. 50–58
