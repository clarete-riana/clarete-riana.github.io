Presents MODC (Memory-Oriented Distributed Computing), a task-based programming framework for disaggregated memory architectures. Achieves resilience through task-level recovery and selective re-execution of failed tasks — drastically reducing mean time to recovery compared to whole-system checkpointing. Particularly relevant for long-running HPC workloads where failures occur after hours of computation.

**Authors:** K. Keeton, S. Singhal, H. Volos, Y. Zhang, R.C. Chaurasiya, C.R. Crasta, et al.

**Venue:** Workshop on Resource Disaggregation and Serverless (WORDS 2021); arXiv:2109.05329
